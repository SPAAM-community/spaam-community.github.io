This year, SPAAM will hold the first elections for the Steering Committee. Following the [constitution](https://www.spaam-community.org/constitution/), the Steering Committee should consist of 12 members with terms of 2 years tiered in two cohorts overlapping for one year, so in the future every year, 6 new members will be elected into the Steering Committee.

Since we are in the process of introducing the new system, this year we will elect only 3 new members into the Steering Committee. In 2027, the terms of 6 of the current members will end and we will start the regular election scheme.
 
**Deadline: August 15th**
